Data visualizations
 
**Candlestick chart** — Open, High, Low, Close; best for understanding daily/weekly price movement.
**Line chart** — Closing price over time; best for simple long-term trends.
**Volume bars** — Shows trading activity and helps identify unusually active periods.
**Moving averages** — Add 20/50/200-day averages to identify trends.
**Relative-performance chart** — Compare a stock against an index or competitors.
**Drawdown chart** — Shows how far the stock has fallen from its previous peak.
**Scatter plot** — Compare risk vs. return across multiple companies.
**Bar chart** — Compare revenue, profit, EPS, P/E, or market cap between companies.
**Heatmap** — Quickly compare many stocks/sectors by daily or monthly return.
