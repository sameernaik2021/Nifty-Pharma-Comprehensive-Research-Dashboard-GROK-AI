1. **Revenue growth** — historical and segment-wise.
2. **EBITDA/operating margins** — stability and trajectory.
3. **ROCE & ROE** — preferably through the cycle.
4. **Free cash flow** — consistency and conversion from profit.
5. **Debt & interest coverage** — leverage, refinancing risk.
6. **Working capital** — receivables, inventory, cash-conversion cycle.
7. **Product mix** — branded, generic, specialty, API, CDMO, OTC.
8. **Geographic mix** — India/US/Europe/emerging markets.
9. **Drug pipeline** — molecules, indications, development stage.
10. **Patent/IP position** — patent expiry, litigation, exclusivity.
11. **Regulatory record** — USFDA/EMA/MHRA observations, warning letters, import alerts.
12. **R&D spending & productivity** — R&D as % sales and meaningful pipeline output.
13. **ANDA/approval pipeline** — particularly important for generics.
14. **Pricing pressure** — especially US generics.
15. **Market size & growth** for major therapies.
16. **Competition** — number/strength of competing drugs.
17. **Concentration risk** — dependence on a few products/customers/markets.
18. **Manufacturing capacity & utilization**.
19. **Quality/compliance systems** — recalls, deviations, regulatory remediation.
20. **API/raw-material dependence** — China/import exposure and backward integration.
21. **Acquisitions/JVs** — price paid, strategic rationale, integration.
22. **Management/promoter quality** — capital allocation, governance, related-party transactions.
23. **Promoter holding/pledging** and institutional ownership.
24. **Employee/ESOP dilution** and share-count growth.
25. **Contingent liabilities** — litigation, tax, regulatory claims.
26. **Earnings quality** — one-offs, exceptional items, accounting changes.
27. **Cash vs reported profit** — investigate persistent divergence.
28. **Valuation** — P/E, EV/EBITDA, P/S, FCF yield versus history and peers.
29. **PEG/growth-adjusted valuation** — only alongside business quality.
30. **Scenario analysis** — base/bull/bear cases for approvals, pricing, margins and pipeline.
31. **Currency exposure** — especially USD/INR.
32. **Dividend/buyback policy** and capital allocation.
33. **Capacity expansion/capex requirements**.
34. **Biosimilar/specialty-drug opportunity**, where relevant.
35. **Patent cliff/revenue-at-risk analysis** over the next 3–5 years.
36. **Insider/promoter transactions** and auditor qualifications.
37. **Corporate governance** — board independence and disclosure quality.
38. **Industry cycle & policy changes** — drug pricing, reimbursement, healthcare regulation.
39. **ESG/environmental compliance** where financially material.
40. **Thesis-breakers** — identify what specific developments would invalidate the investment thesis.
